AeroSentinel Facility Zones SOP

The facility is divided into four authorized inspection zones. The
drone must not enter any area outside these four configured zones.

1. North Gate (north_gate)
A perimeter checkpoint on the north side of the facility. This is a
security-sensitive access point. Any person or vehicle detected here
should be treated as a potential security concern and reviewed
carefully, especially outside scheduled patrol hours.

2. Ground Area (ground_area)
The open yard and field area used for general perimeter awareness and
routine patrol. Generally lower risk than the gates or the warehouse,
but any unexplained activity should still be logged and reviewed.

3. Main Gate (main_gate)
The primary vehicular and pedestrian entrance to the facility. This is
a high-traffic zone; vehicle activity here is expected during normal
business hours and is not automatically suspicious. Unattended
vehicles, forced entry indicators, or activity outside authorized
hours should be treated as elevated risk.

4. Warehouse (warehouse)
The storage and inventory building. This is a restricted-access zone.
Any person or vehicle detected here outside scheduled operations
should be treated as a high-priority security concern and escalated
promptly.

General rules across all zones:

Visual confirmation is required before escalating any suspected
intrusion, regardless of which zone it occurs in.

Medium-risk detections require human review before an incident
response is executed. High-confidence detections should be presented
to the human operator together with the image analysis evidence.

If a mission requests inspection of a location outside these four
zones, it must be rejected during pre-flight safety checks.
