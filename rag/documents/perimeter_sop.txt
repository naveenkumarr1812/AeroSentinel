AeroSentinel Perimeter Security SOP

North perimeter, south perimeter, and east gate are authorized inspection zones.

The north perimeter is considered a restricted security area.

Person detection near a restricted perimeter should be treated as a potential security incident.

Visual confirmation is required before escalating a suspected intrusion.

Medium-risk detections require human review before an incident response is executed.

High-confidence detections should be presented to the human operator with the image analysis evidence.

The drone must not enter areas outside the configured geofence.
