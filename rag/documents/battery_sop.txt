AeroSentinel Battery Safety SOP

Drone missions must maintain sufficient battery reserve.

Minimum battery for mission launch: 30%.

If battery is below 30%, the mission must not be launched.

For missions involving long-distance inspection, the preferred battery level is 50% or higher.

If battery falls below the safe operational threshold during a mission, the drone should terminate the mission and return to its home location.

The safety system must prioritize safe return over mission completion.
