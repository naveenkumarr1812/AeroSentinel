AeroSentinel Mission Operations SOP

Every autonomous mission follows these stages:

1. Understand the mission objective.
2. Select an available drone.
3. Perform pre-flight safety checks.
4. Navigate to the authorized mission location.
5. Capture visual data.
6. Analyze the captured data using the vision system.
7. Assess the detected risk.
8. Request human approval when required.
9. Execute the approved response.
10. Return the drone safely to its home location.

Autonomous actions must not bypass safety checks.

If a mission cannot be completed safely, the drone should return home.
