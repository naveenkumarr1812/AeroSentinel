AeroSentinel Mission Operations SOP

Every autonomous mission follows these stages:

1. Understand the mission objective and target inspection zone.
2. Retrieve relevant operational knowledge and prior mission history
   for that zone.
3. Select an available drone from the fleet.
4. Perform pre-flight safety checks (battery, altitude, geofence).
5. Request human launch approval before the drone takes off. The
   drone does not depart until an operator explicitly approves the
   flight.
6. Navigate to the authorized inspection zone and capture a photo.
7. Analyze the captured photo using the vision system.
8. Present the photo and analysis to a human operator for review.
   This review happens after every single photo, regardless of risk
   level — it is not limited to flagged or high-risk detections.
9. The operator chooses one of two actions:
   a. Request another photo of the same zone (no re-launch — the
      drone is already airborne and simply captures the next
      available image at that location), which repeats from step 7.
   b. Send the drone home, which ends the mission.
10. On return, record the full mission outcome — every photo
    reviewed, the risk assessment for each, and the operator's final
    decision — to persistent memory, and return the drone safely to
    base.

Autonomous actions must not bypass either human checkpoint: launch
approval before departure, and photo review after every capture. If a
mission cannot be completed safely at any stage, the drone must return
home rather than continue.
