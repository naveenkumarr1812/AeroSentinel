AeroSentinel Emergency Response SOP

Potential security incidents must be classified by risk.

Low-risk detections may be logged without immediate escalation.

Medium-risk detections require human approval before an incident response.

High-risk detections should immediately notify the designated security operator and request human confirmation before consequential physical actions.

If communication with the operator is unavailable, the drone should prioritize safety and return home.

The system must maintain an auditable record of significant incidents and operator decisions.
