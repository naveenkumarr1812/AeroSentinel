AeroSentinel Emergency Response SOP

Every photo captured during a mission is reviewed by a human operator
before the drone takes any further action — this applies regardless
of the assessed risk level. Photo review is not conditional on a
detection being flagged; it is a standard step in every mission.

Risk level guides how the operator should treat what they are seeing,
not whether they see it:

None or low risk: routine. The operator can send the drone home or
request another angle purely for situational awareness.

Medium risk: the operator should look closely before deciding. A
request for another photo (a different angle or a closer look) is
often appropriate before returning the drone home.

High risk: the operator should treat this as a priority. If the
scene is ambiguous, request another photo before deciding; if the
detection is clear, return the drone home promptly so the incident
can be acted on and recorded without delay.

If communication with the operator is delayed, the drone should
remain in a safe holding state rather than taking any autonomous
action on its own — a human decision is always required before the
drone proceeds, whether that means requesting another photo or
returning home.

The system must maintain an auditable record of every mission: each
photo taken, its risk assessment, and the operator's decision at each
step. This record is what later missions draw on as prior history for
the same zone.
